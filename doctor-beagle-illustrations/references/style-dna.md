# Style DNA

## One Sentence

Pure white, minimal, hand-drawn, spacious, restrained, strange, product-sketch-like, readable but not instructional.

It should feel like someone who works with AI, product design, systems, and writing quickly drew a sharp explanation on a blank sheet of paper.

## Required

- 16:9 horizontal article illustration.
- Pure white background: no beige, warm gray, paper texture, gradient, shadow, noise, or vintage paper feeling.
- Minimalist black hand-drawn line art: thin lines, slightly wobbly, not mechanical, not perfect vector art, not heavy outlines.
- Lots of blank space: main subject around 40%-60% of the canvas; at least 35% empty white space.
- Sparse handwritten notes: at most 5-8 notes; keep each note short.
- One image explains one core action, structure, state, or metaphor.
- The structure should emerge naturally from the scene. Do not write structure names on the image.

## Color

- Black: main line art, Doctor Beagle outlines, objects, frames, and primary notes.
- Tan/brown: Doctor Beagle ears, head patches, body patches, and tail patch only.
- Red: key problems, warnings, emphasis, or results.
- Orange: main flow, path, arrow, thread, lever, or movement from A to B.
- Blue: secondary notes, feedback, system state, or assistant/automation hints.

Blue is optional. Use color sparingly.

## Hard Bans

- No commercial illustration.
- No PPT infographic.
- No formal flowchart.
- No course slide.
- No cute dog sticker, pet mascot poster, or children's cartoon.
- No complex architecture diagram.
- No polished flat vector illustration.
- No sci-fi UI or real app screenshot.
- No complex background, gradient, shadow, texture, or paper grain.
- No title in the top-left corner, such as "Workflow", "System Map", "Common Pitfalls", or "Roadmap".
- No Simplified Chinese unless the user explicitly asks for it.

## Aesthetic Direction

Make it strange, clever, clean, memorable, and light.

Avoid childish cuteness, decorative pet energy, over-complex systems, and rigid diagrams.
