# Doctor Beagle IP

## Definition

Doctor Beagle is the recurring visual IP for this skill.

Every generated article illustration should include Doctor Beagle by default. Doctor Beagle is not a sticker, pet mascot, or corner decoration. It is a serious little operator doing the strange conceptual work that explains the article.

## Appearance

- Compact small beagle.
- Short legs, low body, small tail.
- Large rounded floppy ears.
- Tiny black dot eyes.
- Black oval nose.
- White muzzle and belly.
- Tan/brown ears, head patches, side patch, and tail patch.
- Slightly uneven hand-drawn outline.
- Deadpan, calm, serious expression.
- No complex clothing, no medical coat by default, no accessories unless the user asks.

## Character

- Serious, patient, slightly absurd.
- Cute enough to be memorable, but not childish.
- Like a low-key system operator in a blank-paper sketch.
- It works hard inside a metaphor: pulling, carrying, sniffing, pushing, sorting, guarding, repairing, or waiting.

## Common Duties

Let Doctor Beagle carry the core action:

- pull an orange thread or broken path
- carry a blank paper box
- sniff a trail of signal dots
- push a lever
- guard a gate
- sort small tokens
- sit beside a strange machine as the operator
- tug a loose process thread
- hold a tiny blank tag in its mouth
- lie flat when the concept is stuck, tired, or overloaded

## Visual Anchors

Use these assets when character fidelity matters:

- `assets/reference/beagle-model-sheet.png`: proportions and views.
- `assets/reference/beagle-action-poses.png`: work actions.
- `assets/reference/beagle-detail-sheet.png`: head shape, ears, patch placement, and palette.

If the generated beagle loses the short legs, floppy ears, black dot eyes, white muzzle, tan patches, or deadpan expression, regenerate with the visual anchors.

## Bans

- Do not generate the old Xiaohei black creature.
- Do not turn Doctor Beagle into a realistic dog.
- Do not turn it into a plush toy, emoji, sticker, commercial mascot, or children's cartoon.
- Do not add shiny eyes, expressive anime face, costumes, hats, medical props, or lab coats unless explicitly requested.
- Do not let Doctor Beagle merely stand in the corner.
- Do not let Doctor Beagle become more important than the conceptual structure.

## Quality Test

If removing Doctor Beagle leaves the image's core metaphor fully intact, Doctor Beagle is too decorative. Rewrite the prompt so Doctor Beagle performs the key conceptual action.
